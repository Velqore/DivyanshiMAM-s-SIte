Academic Profile & Journey

Transforming environmental challenges into sustainable materials solutions through rigorous scientific inquiry and interdisciplinary research.

Dr. Divyanshi Mangla is an Assistant Professor of Chemistry at the School of Basic and Applied Sciences, K.R. Mangalam University, Gurugram. Her research is focused on developing green and sustainable functional materials, biopolymer-based adsorbents, and magnetic nanocomposites for the mitigation of persistent wastewater contaminants.

She completed her Ph.D. in Chemistry from the prestigious Jamia Millia Islamia, New Delhi (Nov 2023). Her doctoral investigations centered on fabricating integrated biopolymer frameworks for removing recalcitrant pharmaceutical residues, especially broad-spectrum antibiotics such as amoxicillin, tetracycline, and trimethoprim, from industrial and municipal effluents.

A distinguished scholar, she qualified the highly competitive CSIR-UGC NET-JRF with an outstanding All India Rank 22 (June 2024), alongside previously qualifying CSIR-UGC NET-LS (June 2019). Her work bridges fundamental adsorption thermodynamics, kinetics, fixed-bed column dynamics, and eco-sustainable nanomaterials synthesis.

Research Program

Core Research Thrusts

Pioneering sustainable chemistry methods to create functional materials that solve urgent water contamination and environmental remediation challenges.

🌱

Biopolymers & Chitosan

Developing novel macromolecular matrices using natural polymers including chitosan and polyvinylpyrrolidone (PVP) for targeted capture of hazardous aqueous contaminants.

Chitosan PVP CMC
🧲

Magnetic Nanocomposites

Engineering superparamagnetic ferrite nanoparticles (NiFe₂O₄, ZnFe₂O₄, CoFe₂O₄) incorporated inside polymeric scaffolds for ultra-fast magnetic separation and reuse.

Magnetic Separation Ferrites Regeneration
🧪

Bio-adsorbents & Carbonization

Harnessing agricultural residues through base-activated hydrothermal carbonisation of coconut husk and wheat straw to create high-surface-area porous carbon adsorbents.

Hydrothermal Carbonisation Agri-waste Biochar
💧

Wastewater Remediation

Conducting in-depth equilibrium isotherm modeling (Langmuir, Freundlich), kinetic modeling, thermodynamic analyses, and fixed-bed continuous flow column dynamics for water purification.

Isotherm Modeling Continuous Column Water Reuse
💊

Antibiotic Capture

Mitigating the global threat of antimicrobial resistance by developing specialized sorbents that eliminate trace antibiotics (Amoxicillin, Tetracycline, Trimethoprim) from aquatic ecosystems.

Amoxicillin Tetracycline Trimethoprim
♻️

Green & Circular Chemistry

Green synthesis protocols integrating natural botanical extracts (Matcha green tea, Ocimum sanctum) for sustainable nanocomposite manufacturing with minimal ecological footprint.

Matcha Green Tea Tulsi / Ocimum Circular Design
