Academic Journey

Dedicated to advancing sustainable chemistry, green functional materials, and high-impact environmental remediation.

Dr. Divyanshi Mangla

Assistant Professor of Chemistry at the School of Basic and Applied Sciences, K.R. Mangalam University, Gurugram (September 2024 – Present). Her research centers on designing next-generation functional biopolymers, magnetic nanocomposites, and low-impact bio-sorbents for aquatic purification.

She completed her Ph.D. in Chemistry from Jamia Millia Islamia, New Delhi (November 2023), investigating biopolymeric systems for removing hazardous antibiotics from wastewater. A distinguished academician, she secured All India Rank 22 in CSIR-UGC NET-JRF (June 2024).

Research Program

Core Research Thrusts

Pioneering sustainable chemistry methods to create functional materials that solve urgent water contamination challenges.

🌱

Biopolymers & Chitosan

Designing advanced functional matrices utilizing chitosan and polyvinylpyrrolidone (PVP) for high-affinity capture of toxic aqueous pollutants.

ChitosanPVPCMC
🧲

Magnetic Nanocomposites

Synthesizing superparamagnetic ferrite nanoparticles (NiFe₂O₄, ZnFe₂O₄) inside biopolymeric scaffolds for facile magnetic recovery and reuse.

Ferrite NPsMagnetic Recovery
🧪

Bio-adsorbents & Carbonization

Upcycling agricultural biomass (coconut husk, wheat straw) via base-activated hydrothermal carbonisation into porous, high-surface-area sorbents.

Hydrothermal CarbonAgri-Waste
💧

Wastewater Remediation

Modeling equilibrium isotherms (Langmuir, Freundlich), kinetics, and continuous fixed-bed dynamic column systems for scalable purification.

Fixed-Bed ColumnAdsorption Kinetics
💊

Antibiotic Capture

Combating antimicrobial resistance by isolating pharmaceutical contaminants including Amoxicillin, Tetracycline, and Trimethoprim from water.

AmoxicillinTetracyclineTrimethoprim
♻️

Green & Circular Chemistry

Integrating green botanical extracts (Matcha green tea, Ocimum sanctum) for eco-friendly synthesis of functional nanomaterials.

Matcha TeaOcimum SanctumEco-Synthesis
