About

Academic profile

Dr. Divyanshi Mangla is an Assistant Professor of Chemistry at K.R. Mangalam University, Gurugram. Her research focuses on sustainable functional materials for environmental remediation, especially biopolymer-based adsorbents, magnetic nanocomposites and bio-derived materials for the removal of antibiotics and organic pollutants from water.

She earned her Ph.D. in Chemistry from Jamia Millia Islamia, New Delhi, with a doctoral thesis titled “Bio-Polymer Based Designing of Integrated System for the Removal of Antibiotics From Wastewater.” She qualified CSIR-UGC NET-JRF with All India Rank 22.

Her work spans adsorption science, polymer–nanomaterial interfaces, wastewater treatment, sustainable chemistry and environmental materials design.

Research

Research interests & themes

My research connects materials chemistry with practical environmental challenges, particularly the design of efficient and reusable systems for water purification.

01

Biopolymers

Chitosan and other polymeric matrices for sustainable functional materials and pollutant capture.

02

Nanocomposites

Magnetic and metal-oxide nanocomposites engineered for separation, adsorption and environmental applications.

03

Bio-adsorbents

Low-impact, biomass-derived and hybrid adsorbents for water and wastewater treatment.

04

Wastewater Remediation

Adsorption, treatment optimization, regeneration and real-water applications for emerging contaminants.

05

Antibiotic Adsorption

Removal of antibiotics such as amoxicillin, tetracycline and trimethoprim from aqueous systems.

06

Sustainable Materials

Designing reusable materials with attention to practical performance, circularity and environmental responsibility.
