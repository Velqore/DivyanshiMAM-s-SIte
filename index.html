Academic Journey & Focus

Dedicated to advancing sustainable chemistry, green functional materials, and high-impact environmental water purification.

Dr. Divyanshi Mangla is an Assistant Professor of Chemistry at the School of Basic and Applied Sciences, K.R. Mangalam University, Gurugram. Her research centers on designing next-generation functional biopolymers, magnetic nanocomposites, and low-impact bio-sorbents for aquatic purification.

She completed her Ph.D. in Chemistry from Jamia Millia Islamia, New Delhi (November 2023). Her doctoral thesis investigated integrated biopolymeric systems for removing hazardous antibiotics including amoxicillin, tetracycline, and trimethoprim from wastewater.

A distinguished academician, she secured All India Rank 22 in the national CSIR-UGC NET-JRF examination (June 2024), after previously qualifying CSIR-UGC NET-LS (June 2019). Her work bridges fundamental adsorption thermodynamics, kinetics, fixed-bed column dynamics, and green synthesis methodologies.

Research Program

Core Research Thrusts

Pioneering sustainable chemistry methods to create functional materials that solve urgent water contamination challenges.

🌱

Biopolymers & Chitosan

Designing advanced functional matrices utilizing chitosan and polyvinylpyrrolidone (PVP) for high-affinity capture of toxic aqueous pollutants.

Chitosan PVP CMC
🧲

Magnetic Nanocomposites

Synthesizing superparamagnetic ferrite nanoparticles (NiFe₂O₄, ZnFe₂O₄, CoFe₂O₄) inside biopolymeric scaffolds for facile magnetic recovery and multiple reusability cycles.

Ferrite Nanoparticles Magnetic Recovery
🧪

Bio-adsorbents & Carbonization

Upcycling agricultural biomass (coconut husk, wheat straw) via base-activated hydrothermal carbonisation into porous, high-surface-area sorbents.

Hydrothermal Carbon Agri-Waste
💧

Wastewater Remediation

Modeling equilibrium isotherms (Langmuir, Freundlich), kinetic mechanisms, and continuous fixed-bed dynamic column systems for scalable purification.

Fixed-Bed Column Adsorption Kinetics
💊

Antibiotic Capture

Combating antimicrobial resistance by isolating and neutralizing emerging pharmaceutical contaminants including Amoxicillin, Tetracycline, and Trimethoprim.

Amoxicillin Tetracycline Trimethoprim
♻️

Green & Circular Chemistry

Integrating green botanical extracts (Matcha green tea, Ocimum sanctum) for eco-friendly synthesis of functional nanomaterials.

Matcha Tea Ocimum Sanctum Eco-Synthesis
